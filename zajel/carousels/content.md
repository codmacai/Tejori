# Zajel service carousels: slide content (draft v1)

12 service carousels + 3 series overviews. Each carousel ships as two separate sets, English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL, mirrored layout). Format 1080×1350.

**Slide structure (service carousels)**
1. Cover: service pill, headline with accent phrase in Zajel Green, photo, logo, swipe cue (`Swipe →` / `اسحب ←`)
2. The need
3. What's included: pills with line icons
4. Why Zajel: 3 proof points
5. Result
6. CTA: shared per series (below)

⚑ = claim Zajel must confirm before publishing. **Bold** = accent phrase in Zajel Green.
